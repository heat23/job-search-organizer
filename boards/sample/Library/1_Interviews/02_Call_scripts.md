# Call scripts

Opening lines and questions for the calls you'll have most. `split: bold` in the front matter above lets a line in **bold** start a new question, which suits long answer lists like this one.

## Recruiter screen

**Open the call**

> Thanks for making the time. I read the posting twice and I'd love to hear what the team is building right now.

**If they ask what you're looking for** ✎

> A senior backend or full-stack role on a product team, where I own services end to end and help shape the design.

- *<Stack: Go or Java on the backend, Postgres, some TypeScript.>*
- *<Remote or hybrid; open to an office two days a week.>*

**Questions to ask the recruiter**

1. What's the level, and who would I report to?
2. What does the interview loop look like: coding, system design, a take-home?
3. What size is the team, and who shares the pager?

## Hiring manager

**Open the call**

> I've read about your platform and I have a few questions already. Could you start with what a great first six months looks like for this role?

**If they ask about a weakness**

> I used to go quiet when I was stuck on a hard bug. Now I timebox it and ask for a second pair of eyes after an hour, which usually saves the day.

**If they ask how you pick what to work on**

> I start from the user problem and the numbers: what's slow, what breaks, what costs the most support time. Then I write a one-page proposal and get feedback before I write code.

## Technical rounds

**Before a coding round**

> Repeat the problem back, ask about input size and edge cases, say your plan out loud, then write the code and test it with one normal and one edge case.

**Before a system design round**

> Start with requirements and numbers, sketch the simplest design that works, then find the bottleneck and scale that part. Name the trade-offs as you go.

## Agency recruiter call

**Open the call**

> Thanks for taking the call. I'm looking at senior backend and full-stack roles, and I'd like to understand which of your openings might fit.

**What they'll ask you**

- Your current stack, level and team.
- Why now, and how quickly you can move.
- Which companies you've already applied to, so they don't submit you twice.

### If they ask about pay {.pay}

> I'd rather hear the range for the role first. If it's a fit on scope, I'm confident we'll find a number that works.

**Close**

> What's the best way to stay in touch, and how often do you like to hear from candidates?
