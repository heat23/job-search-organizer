# Recruiter outreach

One block per contact. A recruiter's prep sheet shows the messages for the people at that agency, next to their stage on the People tab. The format: `## Name, Agency`, then a **Connection request** and a **Follow-up message**, each as a quote.

## Jonah Whitfield, Summit & Vale Partners

**Connection request** (under 300 characters)

> Hi Jonah, I'm a senior backend engineer (Go, Postgres, payments) starting to look for my next role. I saw you place engineers at product companies and would value a short call.

**Follow-up message**

> Thanks for connecting. A quick summary: I own our checkout API and cut its p99 latency by more than two thirds this year. I'm looking for a senior role on a product team. Would 15 minutes next week work?

## Elena Marsh, Summit & Vale Partners

**Connection request** (under 300 characters)

> Hi Elena, I'm exploring senior software engineering roles and noticed you work with fintech teams. I'd be glad to be on your radar.

**Follow-up message**

> Thanks for connecting. If any of your openings need someone who likes owning reliable services end to end, I'd love to talk.
